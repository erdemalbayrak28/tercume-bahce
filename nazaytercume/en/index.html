Frequently Asked Questions

Questions
You May Have

We have collected the most common questions about translation, notary and apostille procedures.

A sworn translation is one certified with the signature and stamp of a translator who has taken an oath before a notary. Unlike a regular translation, it is legally valid before official institutions. Courts, notaries, consulates and public bodies only accept translations bearing a sworn translator’s stamp.

An apostille is an international certification that makes a document valid abroad. It is required for official documents such as diplomas, birth certificates, criminal record extracts and powers of attorney to be used in countries party to the Hague Convention. In Türkiye, apostilles are issued by district and provincial governorships; for non-signatory countries, consulate approval is required instead.

A standard ID card, diploma or birth certificate is usually translated the same day, with notary certification completed within one business day. For multi-page technical specifications and contracts, the timeline depends on document volume. Same-day delivery is available for urgent work.

The fee depends on the source and target language pair, the number of pages or characters, the field of expertise (legal, medical, technical) and the delivery time. If notary certification is requested, the notary fee is added separately. Send us a photo of your document and we will share the exact price free of charge.

All our translators are bound by confidentiality agreements. Your documents are processed only for the duration of the translation, are never shared with third parties, and are deleted from our systems after delivery upon your request. Your personal data is processed in accordance with Turkish data protection law (KVKK).

A clear photo or scan of the document is enough for a quote and for the translation itself. However, for procedures requiring notary certification, the notary may request the original or a certified copy. In that case, simply bring it when collecting your translation.

We provide sworn translation in more than 50 language pairs, primarily English, German, French, Arabic, Russian, Persian, Spanish and Italian. For languages not listed, we find a solution through our translator network.

Translations that do not require certification are sent as PDF by e-mail. Notarised, wet-signed documents can be collected from our office or shipped to your address by courier on request.
